About

KovaPDF is a free online PDF workspace. Open a file, pick a tool, and download the result. Every tool works without paying and without creating an account.

Whether you need to fix a typo in a contract, shrink a heavy scan before emailing it, or pull a table out of a bank statement, the goal is the same: get the job done in a few seconds and keep your document private.
